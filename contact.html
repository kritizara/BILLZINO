Get an answer faster

We read every message ourselves and reply within one business day (Monday to Friday). A few details in your first message usually save a round trip.

What to include

  • The email address of your account, if you have one
  • The page you were on (its address) and what you expected to happen
  • Your browser and device, and a screenshot if something looks wrong
  • For a billing question: the plan and the date of the payment

What we can help with

Billing and plans, refunds (see the refund policy), account access, questions about the invoice builder, bug reports and feature ideas. BillZino creates and tracks invoices — we do not process payments and cannot give tax or legal advice, so check local invoicing rules with an accountant or your tax authority.

Quick answers

How fast do you reply?

We reply within one business day. Messages sent at the weekend are answered on the next business day.

Can you recover an invoice I created without an account?

No. The free generator builds the invoice in your browser and nothing is stored on our servers, so save the PDF as soon as you download it. If you use a dashboard account, write to us from your account email.

Where do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use the form above and start the subject with "Bug" or "Idea". Tell us the steps to reproduce the problem or the job you want the feature to do.

Is the message form a live chat?

No. The form opens your own email app with the message pre-filled, so you keep a copy of everything you send us.
