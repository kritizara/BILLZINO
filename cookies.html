Cookie Policy

What we store in your browser, and why it's very little.

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how billzino.com, operated by RITAJAD LLC, uses cookies and similar browser storage. We keep this deliberately minimal — BillZino doesn't run advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts today.

1. Essential local storage

We store your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage, so the site remembers it on your next visit. This isn't a tracking cookie — it never leaves your browser and contains no personal information.

2. Account sessions (once you sign up)

When account login is enabled, we'll use an essential session cookie to keep you signed in and to keep the checkout process secure. Without it, the product simply can't recognize that you're logged in.

3. Analytics — not currently in use

We don't currently run analytics or advertising cookies on billzino.com. If that changes in the future — for example, to understand basic product usage — we'll update this policy first and use only privacy-respecting, consent-appropriate tools.

4. Payment provider cookies

If you upgrade to a paid plan, our payment provider's checkout page may set its own cookies to process the transaction securely. Those are governed by that provider's own policy, not this one — BillZino never sees your card details either way.

5. Controlling storage

You can clear local storage or cookies at any time through your browser's settings; this may reset your theme preference or sign you out. Since we don't rely on non-essential tracking, blocking cookies won't break the core product.

6. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes — most likely, adding analytics — we'll update the date above.
