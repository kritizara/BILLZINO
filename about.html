Business activity: BILLZINO is an online invoicing and billing software platform, operated by RITAJAD LLC. It helps freelancers, small businesses, and growing teams create invoices, manage clients, and track billing — a digital software service, not a physical product store. BillZino does not process payments, extend credit, or offer investment, gambling, cryptocurrency, adult content, or money transmission services of any kind.

Our story

Built out of frustration, not a pitch deck.

Most invoicing tools make you choose: a design tool that has no idea what an invoice needs, or a payment platform that wants a cut of every transaction. We built neither.
01

Why we built this

BillZino is a template-driven invoice builder — drop in your logo, colors, signature, and line items — paired with a dashboard that tracks every invoice you've sent, per client, per company. No digging through a spreadsheet to find out who paid and who didn't.

02

What we're not

BillZino never touches your money. We don't process payments, store card numbers, or sit between you and your client — you invoice them, they pay you directly, however you've already arranged. Our job is making that invoice look like it came from a real studio, and making sure you always know where it stands.

How we think about it

Three things we won't compromise on.

03

No fake payment claims

We'll never dress this up as a payment platform. If a page implies we move your money, that's a bug — tell us and we'll fix it.

04

Your client data stays yours

Invoices, client lists, and company details are private to your account. We don't sell them, and we don't use them for anything but running your dashboard.

05

Built for people who bill, not agencies who bill you

Every template, dashboard view, and interaction is built for freelancers and small teams first — not enterprise procurement checklists.
