Short answer

Both BillZino and Invoice-Generator.com let you create a free invoice without signing up. Invoice-Generator.com has more guides, calculators, document types and an API. BillZino adds a client dashboard — saved invoices, paid/pending/overdue tracking — and 50+ currencies. Choose based on whether you need a document or a record of what you sent.

Where each one wins

Invoice-Generator.com wins on breadth. If you need a purchase order, a timesheet, a credit note, a Spanish interface or an API, it already has them. Its guide library is also much larger, which is why it ranks for hundreds of informational queries.

BillZino wins on the record after the PDF. Most small businesses do not only need to create an invoice — they need to know which invoices are unpaid. BillZino keeps every document in a dashboard, groups clients automatically, and shows paid, pending and overdue at a glance.

Who should use which

  • Use Invoice-Generator.com if you need many document types, a non-English interface, or you are building an integration.
  • Use BillZino if you send invoices every month, want to track who paid, and prefer a modern mobile-first builder with 50+ currencies.
  • Use both if you want a quick one-off document and a separate place to track ongoing clients.

Frequently asked questions

Is BillZino really free?

Yes. Creating and downloading invoices, quotes, tax invoices and receipts is free, with no signup and no watermark. The dashboard is the add-on for saving and tracking.

Does Invoice-Generator.com require an account?

No for creating and downloading a PDF. An account is optional for saving history and accepting payments.

Which one has more currencies?

BillZino supports 50+ currencies. Invoice-Generator.com supports multiple currencies; check its current list.

Can I use both?

Yes. They are separate tools and there is no conflict in using one for a quick document and the other for tracking.

Is this comparison unbiased?

It is published by BillZino, so it is not independent. We show where the competitor is stronger and link to its site so you can check.
